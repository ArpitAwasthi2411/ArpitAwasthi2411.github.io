About Me

Who I Am

I'm Arpit Awasthi from Palampur, Himachal Pradesh — pursuing a B.Tech in CSE (AI & Data Science) at GHEC Bilaspur and a BS in Data Science from IIT Madras, simultaneously.

My work lives at the seam between deep learning and deployment. I don't just train models — I care about whether they actually run: on a microcontroller at the edge, in a pothole-ridden road, on a vest worn by someone's elderly parent.

"I learn by building things that have to actually work — on real hardware, with messy data, under real constraints. The gap between a model that trains well and one that runs in the field is where the interesting problems live."

— Arpit Awasthi

I've published in IEEE, ranked AIR 430 in GATE 2026 (DA stream), and led hardware-software projects from concept to edge deployment. What drives me: the gap between AI research and the people it could actually help.
