The bug that taught me the most

While driving, the motor board kept vanishing for a second and coming back. Software looked fine. I added diagnostics that log every disconnect with the ESP32's own reset reason, then drove the robot and read the pattern: resets lined up with motor current surges pulling the Pi's 5 V USB rail down.

The fix was electrical, not code. I moved the link to the Pi's GPIO UART, powered the ESP32 from its own regulator and added decoupling. The same dips had been starving the LiDAR's motor. Two problems, one root cause.

VestGuard, a vest that calls for help after a fall

Elders in Himalayan villages often live alone, and after a fall the wait for help is the dangerous part. VestGuard is a traditional Himachali vest with two motion sensors along the spine and an ESP32-S3 that decides on the vest itself, then raises an alarm and alerts the family.

Pothole detection on Himachal's mountain roads

Public pothole datasets are mostly flat city roads. I collected and labelled our own set from mountain roads in Himachal, then compared three YOLO generations under identical training to see which one is actually worth deploying.
